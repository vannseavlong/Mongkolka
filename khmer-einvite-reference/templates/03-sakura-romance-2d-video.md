## Visual style
- **Palette:** blush pink, soft lavender, cream/white + vivid fuchsia, magenta, deep purple florals + metallic gold-foil accents.
- **Motifs:** hanging pink cherry-blossom branches along the top border; flower beds (roses, hydrangeas, lavender) along the bottom and sides; ornate Khmer filigree; symmetrical gold crest monogram (`BC`).
- **Background:** soft, misty enchanted hallway / cathedral garden, pink floral pillars, soft light.
- **Particles:** white butterflies, drifting petals, sparkle flares, flying white doves.
- **Typography:** Khmer = classic traditional serif heading in **bold gold gradient with subtle emboss/bevel**; Latin = modern calligraphy for romantic phrases + clean Roman all-caps serif for date/venue/parents.

---

## Scene-by-scene

1. **Opening & monogram (0:00–0:02)** — centered gold monogram with filigree + corner borders; monogram pulses with a light burst that dissolves into the garden scene, doves fly across.
2. **Announcement (0:03–0:09)** — Khmer សិរីមង្គលអាពាហ៍ពិពាហ៍ សូមគោរពអញ្ជើញ fades in, then date + reception venue. Exit via white flash.
3. **Couple intro (0:10–0:17)** — blossoms top, garden bottom, monogram top-center; couple cut-out in traditional attire (groom holding ceremonial betel box, bride with white bouquet) fades into center; Khmer names.
4. **Formal invitation — Khmer (0:18–0:26)** — monogram + header; groom's parents left, bride's parents right; couple names center in gold; date & venue in Khmer at bottom. Cross-dissolve.
5. **Formal invitation — English (0:27–0:34)** — symmetrical: `Mr. … & Mrs. …` both families, couple names in script, date & venue.
6. **Agenda (0:35–0:45)** — 2-day program in gold/brown Khmer serif:
   - Day 1 (Sat 16 Mar): afternoon monk blessing & ritual ceremonies at the residence.
   - Day 2 (Sun 17 Mar): morning procession (ហែជំនូន), hair-cutting (កាត់សក់), wrist-tying (សែនចងដៃ), evening reception 5 PM.
7. **Letter of gratitude (0:46–0:51)** — centered Khmer លិខិតថ្លែងអំណរគុណ thanking guests, dignitaries, relatives.
8. **Pre-wedding gallery (0:52–1:13)** — photos in an elegant gold rectangular filigree frame over the florals. Sequence: traditional attire on a golden swan boat; groom in uniform + bride in gown in nature; walking on a tree-lined road; evening candle-floating on water. Soft wipe cross-fades with sparkles.
9. **Apology note (1:14–1:20)** — លិខិតសូមអភ័យទោស for any missed formal paper invitations.
10. **"Save the Date" climax & outro (1:21–1:30)** — big flowing gold script "Save the Date" + date + venue → vendor end card.

## Audio
Romantic Cambodian acoustic/pop love ballad (acoustic guitar, male vocals). Warm, emotional, celebratory.

## Takeaways
- Gold-gradient + bevel Khmer heading is a strong "premium" signal.
- Frame on all 4 sides (blossom top, flower bed bottom) and keep the center clean for text.
- Both a gratitude letter AND an apology letter as separate sections.
