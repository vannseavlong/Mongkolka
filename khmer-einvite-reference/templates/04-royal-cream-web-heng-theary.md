## UI sections in order

1. **Cover / opening gateway**
   - Ornate gold shield monogram with initials (H & T).
   - Khmer greeting សូមគោរពអញ្ជើញ.
   - **Personalized guest name slot** (ភ្ញៀវកិត្តិយស) — each guest gets their own link.
   - Rounded pill CTA **សូមចុចបើកសំបុត្រ** (Click to open invitation).
   - Gold floral border at the bottom of the cover card.
2. **Hero / couple reveal**
   - Smooth transition → softly lit pre-wedding photo in an elegant floral oval arch, butterfly particle animation.
   - Names in calligraphic Latin "Heng & Theary", date `29 . MAY . 2026` below.
3. **Formal invitation & lineage**
   - Khmer first, English translation after.
   - Groom's parents left, bride's parents right (symmetrical).
   - Traditional respectful invitation to relatives, friends, dignitaries.
   - Date/time + venue.
4. **Featured video / moving portrait** — couple in traditional royal Khmer attire.
5. **Traditional program timeline** — each slot with delicate pink/gold line-art icon:
   - ពិធីហែជំនូន (procession / gift parade)
   - Ring / tea ceremony
   - ពិធីកាត់សក់បង្កក់សិរី (hair-cutting)
   - ពិធីសែនចងដៃ (blessing / knot-tying)
   - ពិធីជប់លៀងពិសាភោជនាហារ (evening reception)
6. **Venue & map** — custom map visual + CTA **ភ្ជាប់ទៅទីតាំង** (open Google Maps).
7. **Photo gallery** — masonry grid (traditional outfits, outdoor, boat scenery).
8. **Digital gift** — Bakong / KHQR code + account name.
9. **Gratitude & apology** — Khmer + English.
10. **Guestbook / wishes** — fields ឈ្មោះ (name) / ជូនពរ (wish), button **ផ្ញើសារជូនពរ**, live feed with timestamps.
11. **Footer** — "In partnership with…" + vendor credits, socials, Telegram.

## Persistent controls
- **Floating bottom dock** (glassmorphism): Home, Couple, Schedule, Location, Gallery, Gift/QR, Guestbook.
- **Music toggle** top-right: spinning vinyl icon.

## Design
- **Palette:** royal gold, warm champagne, soft cream/ivory parchment; accents blush pink, peach florals, rose-gold.
- **Typography:** Khmer = traditional calligraphic serif headers (Moul style) + clean modern Khmer body (Battambang style); Latin = sweeping calligraphic cursive for names + clean serif/sans for dates & labels.
- **Motifs:** European royal crest blended with Khmer ceremonial framing; lush watercolor floral borders (roses, peonies, greenery); ambient bokeh; butterflies.
- **Promo background:** a soft-focus European palace + bride's gown, with the phone held in hand.

## What makes it premium
1. Unfold/opening transition = feels like opening a luxury physical card.
2. Micro-interactions: floating sparkles, rotating music disc, subtle fade-ins — lively but not cluttered.
3. Culture + utility together: full lineage & ritual program **plus** KHQR, maps, live guestbook.
