## Overall theme & design language
- **Concept:** Enchanted garden / royal European estate.
- **Palette:** warm cream/ivory background (`#FDFBF7`), soft champagne gold, metallic gold-foil typography, soft dusty pink, forest green, warm architectural stone tones.
- **Typography:**
  - Latin: classic elegant serif (Cormorant Garamond / Cinzel style) + romantic calligraphy/script for initials and headers.
  - Khmer: rounded/traditional Khmer display font for headings, high-legibility traditional Khmer body text.
- **Decorative elements:** Khmer royal monogram emblem, gold arched borders, floral divider flourishes, vintage garden-gate illustration, classical fountain & palace-estate illustration, flying birds/doves silhouettes.
- **Persistent UI:**
  - Top-right: floating gold audio/music icon.
  - Bottom: semi-transparent glassmorphism dock with gold icons — Agenda (calendar), Map (pin), Gallery (photo grid), Guestbook (speech bubble).

## Scene-by-scene / section-by-section

### 1. Interactive entrance cover (0:00–0:11)
- Gold filigree interlocking monogram (`T & L`).
- Khmer welcome: សូមអញ្ជើញចូលរួមពិធីមង្គលការ (kindly invited to celebrate our wedding).
- Background: illustrated classical palace garden with wrought-iron gate.
- Bottom pill button: **សូមចុចបើកសំបុត្រ** ("Please click to open invitation").
- **On click:** ornate golden gate opens → romantic fountain court + English quote *"Join Us Together Till Eternity"*.

### 2. Formal invitation & parents' blessing (0:12–0:16)
- Traditional symmetrical dual-column Khmer announcement.
- Left: groom's parents (with honorific titles where applicable) + groom's name.
- Right: bride's parents + bride's name.
- Center: gold wedding crest separating both families.
- Bottom: formal Khmer text expressing gratitude and requesting guests' presence.

### 3. English date & countdown (0:17–0:19)
- Centered English card inside an arched gold border.
- Couple names, `THURSDAY 16th JANUARY 2025`, time + venue in caps.
- Header: **ចំនួនថ្ងៃរង់ចាំការមកដល់ / Event Countdown**; status text "Wedding Day – Congratulations" when the day arrives.
- First couple portrait in traditional Khmer golden silk attire (Sampot Chang Kben & Sbai).

### 4. Wedding agenda timeline (0:20–0:28)
- Header: **កម្មវិធីបុណ្យប្រពៃណី / EVENT AGENDA**.
- Vertical timeline, gold icons per segment (floral/palace icons, rings).
- Segments: morning blessings, procession of fruit/gifts (ហែជំនូន), ring exchange, haircut ceremony (កាត់សក់), evening banquet.
- Precise Khmer timestamps from ~6:30 AM to 5:00 PM banquet.

### 5. Location & map (0:29–0:30)
- Header: **ទីតាំងកម្មវិធី / LOCATION**.
- Custom illustrated route map in gold tones with landmarks + venue.
- QR code to Google Maps.
- Button: **ចុចមើលទីតាំង** ("Click to view location").

### 6. Photo gallery (0:31–0:45)
- Header: **កម្រងរូបភាព / PHOTO GALLERY**.
- Asymmetrical 3-column / masonry grid.
- Mix: traditional attire at heritage sites, casual lifestyle shots, white gown / black-tie in modern architecture.
- Tap → full-screen lightbox with smooth zoom.

### 7. Digital gift / QR (0:46–0:47)
- Header: **អំណោយចំណងដៃ / WEDDING GIFT**.
- Illustrated gift box with glowing gold sparks.
- ABA Pay / KHQR code in a gold frame + account name + phone.
- Button: **ចុចដើម្បីចម្លងលេខកុង** ("Click to copy account number").

### 8. Gratitude, apology & guestbook (0:48–0:51)
- **សេចក្តីថ្លែងអំណរគុណ និងសូមអភ័យទោស / OUR GRATITUDE AND APOLOGY** — apology if a physical invitation was missed.
- Guestbook header: **សៀវភៅជូនពរ / GREETING MESSAGE**.
- Inputs: Name, Comment. Button: **ចុចផ្ញើសារជូនពរ** (Submit wish).
- Live public feed of wishes with names + timestamps.

### 9. Footer (0:52–0:56)
- Vendor watermark/branding + social links (Facebook, Instagram, Telegram).
- Reel ends on a real photo of the couple at the actual enchanted-forest wedding stage → shows the invitation theme matched the real decor.

---

## Key takeaways to copy (as technique, not artwork)
- The **gate-opening reveal** is the hook.
- Theme matches the real wedding stage decor → feels "bespoke".
- Bottom dock + music icon make it feel like an app.
