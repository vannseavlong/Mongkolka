## Concept
Whimsical pastel fairytale: chibi baby dragons, magical castle, fairies/pixies, glowing bokeh sparkles.

## Palette
Rose pink `#FF6584`, berry/magenta `#C82A62`, champagne gold `#D4AF37`; backgrounds pastel peach, warm cream `#FFF8F0`, mint, lavender; text dark crimson / charcoal brown on light parchment cards.

## Typography
- Khmer: decorative rounded festive display for headings + clean modern Khmer body.
- Latin: bubbly/whimsical fairytale display for the title ("MOROKOD 1ST BIRTHDAY"); elegant uppercase serif for labels (SAVE THE DATE, EVENT AGENDA, LOCATION, PHOTO GALLERY).

## Characters & motifs
- Trio of chibi dragons: pink princess dragon (gold horns, small wings, big violet eyes) center; mint dragon with party hat left; cream dragon holding a cake right.
- Pastel castle, flying fairies holding a banner, gold sparkles, stars, clouds, bokeh.

## UI sections
A. **Cover:** pixie banner "You Are Invited – Let's Celebrate", big title, pink pill CTA **សូមចុចទីនេះដើម្បីចូល / CLICK HERE TO OPEN**, the 3 mascots at the bottom.
B. **Transition:** sparkle burst → scrollable parchment page; music starts.
C. **Sticky controls:** top-right audio toggle with pulsing waves; bottom pill dock (Invitation, Location, Gallery, Wishes).
D. **Invitation card:** frosted-white rounded card, formal Khmer wording honoring the child, date/time/venue.
E. **Countdown + hero portrait:** live days/hours/min/sec; framed studio photo of the baby in pink & gold traditional Khmer costume.
F. **Agenda:** SAVE THE DATE / របៀបវារៈកម្មវិធី — 5:00 PM reception & welcome; dinner & toast.
G. **Location:** venue exterior photo thumbnail + OPEN LOCATION button.
H. **Gallery:** multi-grid of milestone & studio portraits.
I. **Gratitude:** bilingual thank-you from the parents.
J. **Guestbook:** Your Name / Your Message / SEND GREETING (pink) + live feed with timestamps & emoji.
K. **Footer:** vendor credit + socials.

## Animation
Parallax scroll, fade + scale-in cards, continuous floating stars/sparkles, dock smooth-scroll.

## Audio
Sweet, gentle, joyful acoustic/orchestral lullaby.
