## UI sections & flow
1. **Cover / door screen**
   - Ornate gold archway border framing classic double doors (Art Deco lines + Khmer door paneling, multi-layer gold pin-striping).
   - Center: traditional Khmer medallion emblem reading **ពរជ័យ គេហដ្ឋាន** + host/family name.
   - CTA text: **សូមចុចទីនេះ ដើម្បីបើកការអញ្ជើញ** (Tap here to open the invitation).
2. **Opening transition** — the two emerald/gold doors swing open toward the edges in 3D perspective.
3. **3D emblem reveal** — glowing 3D golden Cambodian emblem rotates in from center with sparkle particles; then the date appears in **Khmer lunar + solar** format:
   - ថ្ងៃពុធ ៣កើត ខែភទ្របទ ឆ្នាំរោង ឆស័ក ព.ស. ២៥៦៨
   - ត្រូវនឹងថ្ងៃទី០៤ ខែកញ្ញា ឆ្នាំ២០២៤
4. **Main scroll page**
   - Gold circular speaker button pinned top-right.
   - Host & greeting: emblem + formal blessing text introducing the hosts.
   - Status badge: "Event Started" (countdown completes into this).
   - **Program (កម្មវិធីបុណ្យ):** chronological schedule (monk chanting, morning offerings, lunch reception), clear time labels (ម៉ោង).
   - **Location (ទីតាំងកម្មវិធី):** embedded map preview + gold button **ចុចមើលទីតាំង**.
   - **Gratitude letter (លិខិតថ្លែងអំណរគុណ).**
   - Floating bottom action buttons (calendar reminder, share, navigation).

## Layout
- Mobile-first portrait; consistent inner gold frame on every screen; strict vertical center axis (formal Cambodian layout).

## Palette
- Background: deep emerald `#0D2B1D` / `#081C13` (prosperity, peace).
- Gold: `#E5C07B`, `#D4AF37`, `#F3E5AB` (honor, blessing, royalty).
- Secondary text: muted gold / warm off-white.

## Typography
- Khmer headings/emblem: **Moul / Moul Light** (ceremonial).
- Khmer body/schedule: **Battambang / Siemreap / Kantumruy Pro** style.
- Latin: refined serif + elegant script for names.

## Motifs
- Traditional **Kbach (ក្បាច់)**: flame/lotus-leaf filigree in the seal, corners, dividers.
- Geometric Art Deco + Khmer door paneling.
- Glow, lens flare, floating gold dust around the medallion.

## Animations
- 3D door swing on tap; continuous particles during opening; continuous slow rotation of the medallion; smooth scroll with sticky sound toggle + bottom actions.

## Why it feels premium
1. High-contrast emerald + metallic gold = regal.
2. 3D door intro makes a link feel exclusive and ceremonial.
3. Traditional festive music = emotional/cultural.
4. Gold map borders, custom icons, refined Khmer type replicate gold-foil stationery.
